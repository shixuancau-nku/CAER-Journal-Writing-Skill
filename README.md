# CAER-Journal-Writing-Skill
A skill which help researchers re-write their journal article as China Agricultural Economic Review form.

This skill aim to reduce the work which transit the orginal journal article into ouher journal's style. In this skill, I choose 11 agriculturual economic journal, which including 6 CAER articles and 5 other AE journal articles (like AJAE etc) to let the AI Agent learning and working.

The whole skill is seprated by 4 part, including the lanch prompt and 3 skill part. Skill_1 is the study result by AI after it was been given the whole sample papers. Skill_2 was the demand that instruct AI Agent to re-write the paper as skill_1. And skill_3 was a checker to make sure the article can be the form of CAER. All output will in markdown form.

The whole orginal work was powered by Opencode, and the inside AI Agent was Deepseek V4 pro.
